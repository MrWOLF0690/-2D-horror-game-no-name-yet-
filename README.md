# THE IRON FOUNDRY(2D HORROR GAME)
It's a horror game made in the style of top down 2D games, about a Detective and some cases he investigates.
Action takes place in an abandoned Iron factory/foundry (some parts of the story will be played outside this space) you a detective investigate a crime scene after the victim's mother has called you worried that weird lights and sounds have been coming from the foundry, it's your job to investigate and find clues about the mistery
